About POLT

POLT Technological Solutions is a registered Nigerian business based in Zaria, Kaduna State, working with businesses across the north of the country and beyond.

We keep the team small and the scope honest: every project is scoped around what will actually move your business forward, not what looks good in a pitch deck. If a simpler build serves you better than a complex one, we'll say so.

Alongside client work, we run programming instruction for people who want to understand and eventually own the systems we build for them.
